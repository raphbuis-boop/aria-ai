FAQ

Answers to what comes up most.

What is Aria?

Aria is an AI-powered CRM built specifically for New Jersey real estate agents. It handles your inbox, your pipeline, your NJMLS matching, your CMAs, and your client communication — in one place, under one login.

Is it really free during beta?

Yes. No credit card, no trial period, no countdown timer. Aria is free for the first 100 NJ agents who join the beta. When paid plans launch, beta members lock in a lifetime discount.

How long does setup take?

Most agents are texting leads through Aria within 20 minutes. You connect your phone number, upload your existing leads, and Aria starts mirroring your voice from your past texts.

Does Aria actually sound like me?

Yes. Aria reads your past texts and emails — your phrases, your sign-offs, your closing style — and writes in your voice. Leads can't tell it's not you, because in every way that matters, it is.

What MLS does Aria support?

NJMLS via SimplyRETS, with real-time listing matching. GSMLS is on the roadmap.

Who built this?

An NJ family of agents who got tired of running one business out of six different tools. Aria is built by people who have sat at a closing table and run a CMA at midnight.
